Questions

What can I read here?

Guides about how movies are made, credits, animation, and planning a movie night. Sample films are not on this site.

Why are movies missing?

Studio films still belong to their studios. VividMovie does not host copies of them, and the sample film pages have been removed.

Who is the site for?

Families who want written guides and legal films. The policy pages explain privacy, cookies, and terms.

How do I ask a question?

Use the contact page or write to abenaaku@gmail.com.
