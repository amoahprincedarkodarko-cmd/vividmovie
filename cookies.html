Cookie notice

Last updated October 8, 2026. A cookie is a small text file a site can store in the browser.

Cookies used now

VividMovie does not set an advertising cookie today. A video you upload is kept in the browser for playback and is not a tracking cookie.

If ads are added

An approved ad service may set cookies to count a visit, remember that an ad was shown, and measure whether the ad loaded. Those cookies belong to the ad service. You can block third-party cookies in the browser settings.

Choice

You can refuse non-essential cookies in your browser. The home page will still open. Questions about cookies can be sent from the contact page.
