What happens after filming

The steps between the last camera day and the finished film.

Post-production is the work after the shoot. The film is not finished when the camera is packed away.

The first step is to copy the files to two places. A lost memory card can erase the project. Label the copies with the date.

Next comes the edit, then sound repair, then color. Color work makes daytime scenes match even if one was filmed under a cloud. Sound repair lowers hiss and evens the volume of voices.

Titles and credits are added last. The opening title should be readable. The end credits should name the people who helped, including anyone who lent a room.

Export a copy your family can play. Keep the project file too, in case you need to fix a name in the credits.
