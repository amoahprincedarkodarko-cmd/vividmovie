Color in movies

How color helps a scene feel warm, cold, or important.

Color is a tool, not only a record of what the room looked like. Filmmakers choose clothes and light so the audience feels the scene.

Warm colors such as orange and yellow often feel friendly or sunny. Cool colors such as blue and green can feel calm or uncertain. A film can use both, as long as the choice is repeated on purpose.

One strong color can mark a character. A blue coat in a gray street is easy to follow. If every person wears a bright color, no one stands out.

On a phone camera, avoid mixed light when you can. A window plus a yellow lamp can turn faces odd colors. Pick one main light.

You do not need a filter to use color. A red notebook on a wooden table is already a color choice.
