Mr. Bug Goes to Town (1941)

This is the real full film, about 78 minutes. It plays here from the Internet Archive. It is in the public domain.

Hoppity the grasshopper comes home and finds his insect neighbors squeezed by the growing city. Fleischer Studios made this film.
