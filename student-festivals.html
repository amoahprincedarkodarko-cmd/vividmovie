Student film festivals

How a class film can be shown to people outside the classroom.

A student festival is a screening for films made by students. Some are inside one school. Some accept films from many schools.

Read the rules before you edit. A festival may limit the length, require a teacher’s approval, and ask you to confirm that music and pictures are allowed. Those rules are part of the entry, not an extra.

Make a short description of two or three sentences. Say the title, the length, and the problem in the story. Do not reveal the ending.

Export the film in the format the festival requests. A file that will not open cannot be judged. Test it on a second computer.

A rejection is normal. Festivals have more entries than seats. Keep the notes, fix one clear problem, and enter another screening later.
