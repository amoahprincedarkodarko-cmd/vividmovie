Legal ways to watch a film

Tickets, discs, libraries, and paid services — and what does not count.

A legal viewing is one the rights owner allows. The usual paths are a cinema ticket, a disc your family owns, a library loan, or a service the family pays for.

A free film can still be legal. Some old films are in the public domain. Some creators post their own work for free. The difference is permission, not the price.

A site that offers new theater films for free is not one of these paths. The studio did not give that site the film. Watching there does not pay the people who made it.

If you make a film, you may show it to your class and family. Posting it online is fine when the pictures, music, and voices are yours or permitted.

When you are unsure, ask a parent, a teacher, or a librarian before you download. That question is the safe step.
