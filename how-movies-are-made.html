How a movie is made

A film is not one person with a camera. It is a long set of jobs that fit together.

The idea

Someone writes a story. The pages are called a script. The script lists what people say and what the camera should see. A short film can start as a school story. A long film can take months to write.

Planning

The director decides how the story should feel. A producer finds the money, the places to film, and the people who will work. Artists draw the scenes before they are filmed. Those drawings are called storyboards.

Filming

Actors learn their lines. A camera operator records the scene. A sound person records voices and noises. The same moment may be filmed many times so the editor can choose the clearest take.

Finishing

The editor joins the best pieces in order. Musicians add a score. Color and sound are cleaned up. Only then is the film ready for a theater, a disc, or a legal website.

VividMovie does not host films that belong to a studio. A creator can share a film they own. An old film can be shown when it is in the public domain.
