Music in film

How a score supports a scene without replacing it.

A score is music written for the film. A song is a piece with words that may play over a scene. Both can help, and both can get in the way.

Music can tell the audience that a moment is safe, funny, or uncertain. It should follow the picture. A loud chase theme over a quiet kitchen makes the scene confusing.

Many student films use no score at all. Footsteps and voices are enough. Add music only where the picture needs a lift, such as the start or the end.

The rights question matters. A famous song is owned by someone. Playing it in your room is not the same as putting it in a film you post online. Use music you made, music marked free to use, or music you have permission to include.

If you write a simple tune on a keyboard, keep the file with the project. That file proves the music is yours.
