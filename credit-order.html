Why credit order matters

How films list names, and how a school film can do it fairly.

Credit order is the sequence of names at the end of a film. On a large film, contracts can decide who appears first. On a school film, fairness is the better rule.

List the main actors, then the director, camera, sound, editor, and anyone who wrote the script. Add a thank-you line for a parent who drove or a teacher who lent a room.

Use the name each person asks you to use. Check the spelling with them before you export. A wrong name in the credits is a hard mistake to undo after you share the file.

Do not list a famous actor who is not in your film. A credit is a statement that the person worked on it.

Keep the credits on screen long enough to read. A fast scroll looks unfinished. Three to five seconds for a short list is enough.
