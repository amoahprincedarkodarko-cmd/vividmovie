What public domain means

Public domain means the copyright has ended, or the work was given to the public. People may copy and show that film without asking a studio.

Most new movies are not in the public domain. The company that paid for the film still owns it. Putting that film on a website without a license is not allowed, even if the site also shows ads.

Some films from the 1920s and 1930s are in the public domain in the United States. A site may show those copies. VividMovie does not keep sample film players on this website.

A public-domain film can still have a new score or a new restoration that someone else owns. The safe copy is the one an archive lists as public domain.
