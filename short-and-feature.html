Short films and feature films

How a five-minute film differs from a film that fills an evening.

A short film usually runs under about 40 minutes. Many school films are 2 to 8 minutes. A feature film is long enough for an evening, often around 90 minutes or more.

A short film has room for one problem. A feature has room for several problems that connect. Trying to fit a feature plot into five minutes makes the story feel rushed.

Time also changes the crew. A short film can be made by a few people in a weekend. A feature needs many days, more actors, and a schedule that survives sick days and rain.

Both kinds of film use the same tools: a script, shots, sound, and an edit. Learning those tools on a short film is the usual path. The short film is not a lesser film. It is a complete story in a smaller frame.

When you plan, pick the length first. Then cut the plot until it fits that length.
