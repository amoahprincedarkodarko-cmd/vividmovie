Gulliver’s Travels (1939)

This is the real full film, about 76 minutes. It plays here from the Internet Archive. It is in the public domain.

A traveler washes ashore in Lilliput. The people are tiny, and two kingdoms argue about a wedding song. Gulliver helps them settle it, then sails home.
