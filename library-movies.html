Borrowing films from a library

A legal way to watch movies without buying each one.

A public library often lends films on disc or through an app the library pays for. That loan is a legal way to watch.

Ask a librarian which service the library uses. Some apps need a library card number. The loan ends after a set number of days, and the film stops playing. That end date is part of the agreement.

A library copy is for your household’s viewing. It is not a file to upload to a website or pass to a group chat. When the loan ends, the viewing ends.

If the film you want is out, place a hold. The library will tell you when it is ready. That wait is better than searching for a copy that is not licensed.

Library staff can also help you find a film that fits a family night. Tell them the age of the viewers and the kind of story you want.
