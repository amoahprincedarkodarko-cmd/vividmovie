How to read the credits

The names at the end of a film are the people who made it. Each title is a job.

The director guides the actors and decides how scenes should look. The writer creates the script. The producer organizes the work and the budget. The director of photography is in charge of the camera and light.

The editor chooses which filmed moments stay in the story. The composer writes the music. Costume and set designers build the clothes and rooms. Voice actors speak for drawn characters.

A credit does not mean that person uploaded the film to a website. The studio or the rights owner decides where the film can be shown. Reading the credits is a good way to see how many people a movie takes.
