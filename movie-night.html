Plan a family movie night

A movie night works better with a plan. Pick the film before the evening starts.

Choose a film everyone is allowed to watch. A film from a library, a disc your family owns, or a paid streaming service is a fair choice.

Set a start time and a seat for each person. Keep the room dark enough to see the picture and light enough to walk safely. Pause if someone needs a break.

After the film, ask one question: what choice did a character make, and what happened next? That turns the night into a conversation instead of only a screen.

Do not download a film from a site that does not have the right to share it. A free copy of a new movie is usually not a legal copy.
