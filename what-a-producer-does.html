What a producer does

How a producer plans the people, places, and schedule for a film.

A producer organizes the work that lets a film get made. If the director guides the scenes, the producer guides the plan around those scenes.

The producer lists who is needed: actors, a camera person, a sound person, and someone to watch the time. For a class project, the producer can be the student who books the room and reminds everyone when to meet.

Money is part of the job on a larger film. The producer estimates costumes, food for the crew, and the cost of a location. A school film may have no budget. The producer still decides what can be borrowed and what must be left out.

When something goes wrong, the producer finds a new time or a new place. Rain can cancel an outdoor scene. A missing prop can stop a shot. The schedule has to bend without losing the story.

The producer is also the person who checks that the film uses music and pictures the team has permission to use. That check protects the finished movie.
