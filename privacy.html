Privacy policy

Last updated October 8, 2026. This policy explains what VividMovie does with information when you use the site.

Videos you choose

If you upload a video, the file stays in your browser on that device. VividMovie does not send that file to a server and does not publish it for other people.

Information the site may receive

If you write through the contact form, the message includes the name, email address, and note you type. Use that information only to reply. If advertising is added later, the ad provider may use a cookie to count visits and show ads, as described on the cookie page.

Children

VividMovie is a family site. Do not send private details such as a home address, school name, or phone number in a video title or contact message.

Choices

You can remove an uploaded video by closing the page or clearing the browser. You can ask a privacy question from the contact page.
