About VividMovie

VividMovie is a family video website. People can keep a small shelf of videos they made or have permission to use. Those files stay on the device. They are not published to the rest of the world.

The site also explains how a legal movie website works. A creator may share a film they own. Viewers watch it on the site. If advertising is approved later, ads would appear only on pages with real information, and earnings would belong to the site owner who is allowed to hold the ad account.

VividMovie does not offer copies of studio films. Titles from theaters and streaming services are not hosted here. The home shelf starts empty on purpose.

Who it is for

The site is for families who want a simple place to play their own clips, school projects, and other videos they are allowed to share. It is not a download library for movies made by other people.

What you will find

  • A home page with an upload button for your own video.
  • An about page that explains the site.
  • A contact page for questions.
  • A privacy policy, a cookie notice, and terms of use.
